🎶 Join Us for a Summer of Music

The Pleasant Hill Civic Action Commission proudly presents the Sunset by the Lake Summer Concert Series — a beloved community tradition bringing free live music to Pleasant Hill every summer.

Bring your blankets, lawn chairs, and picnic baskets. Food trucks and beverage vendors will be on-site at every concert.

🎪 Family Fun Zones — Six concerts this year feature pre-show Family Fun Zones with activities for kids including bounce houses and more!

📍 Location: Pleasant Hill City Hall, 100 Gregory Lane
🕕 Time: 6:00 PM – 8:00 PM, every two weeks
🎟️ Admission: FREE for everyone!
Good to Know

Frequently Asked Questions

When and where are the concerts?
Every other Sunday from 6:00 PM to 8:00 PM, May 24 through August 30, 2026, on the lawn at Pleasant Hill City Hall, 100 Gregory Lane, Pleasant Hill, CA 94523. Get directions.
How much do the concerts cost?
Nothing — admission is completely free for all ages. No tickets or reservations needed.
What should I bring?
Blankets, lawn chairs, and picnic baskets are all encouraged. Food trucks and beverage vendors are on-site at every concert, so you can also just show up hungry.
Is there parking?
Yes — free parking is available at Pleasant Hill City Hall and in the surrounding areas.
Are the concerts family-friendly?
Absolutely. Six of the eight 2026 concerts feature a pre-show Family Fun Zone with kids' activities including bounce houses, and the whole series is free and open to all ages.
