About the Band

The Big Jangle is an East Bay tribute band that has been recreating the Tom Petty and The Heartbreakers concert experience since 2012. Beyond Petty's catalog, they perform songs he covered or played live — The Traveling Wilburys, The Byrds, and Stevie Nicks all make appearances.

A true series favorite, this is their fourth straight year closing out a Pleasant Hill summer (2023, 2024, 2025 — and now the 2026 season finale). Come sing "Free Fallin'" into the sunset one more time.

About This Concert

The Big Jangle closes out the 2026 Sunset by the Lake Summer Concert Series. Come send the summer off in style!

What to bring: Blankets, lawn chairs, and picnic baskets are encouraged. Food trucks and beverage vendors will be available on-site.

Parking: Free parking is available at Pleasant Hill City Hall and surrounding areas.
