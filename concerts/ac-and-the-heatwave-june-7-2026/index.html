About the Band

AC & the Heatwave is an East Bay band that puts its own flair on funk, soul, and R&B classics. Their high-energy, danceable sets span generations — from Sly & the Family Stone, Stevie Wonder, and The Temptations through modern hits like Bruno Mars.

Bring your dancing shoes: this one is built to get the lawn moving before the sun goes down.

About This Concert

The 2026 Sunset by the Lake Summer Concert Series continues with AC & the Heatwave!

This concert features a Family Fun Zone with activities for kids including bounce houses and more—arrive early to enjoy the fun before the music starts!

What to bring: Blankets, lawn chairs, and picnic baskets are encouraged. Food trucks and beverage vendors will be available on-site.

Parking: Free parking is available at Pleasant Hill City Hall and surrounding areas.
