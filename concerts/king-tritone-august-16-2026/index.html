About the Band

King Tritone is a Bay Area cover-rock band — Jim Raines, Tom Fleming, Eric Jones, and John Messier — playing rock covers spanning the 1970s to today along with some originals.

They're local in the truest sense: the band gigs regularly around central Contra Costa County, including repeat appearances right here in Pleasant Hill at WiseGirl Ristorante.

About This Concert

The 2026 Sunset by the Lake Summer Concert Series continues with King Tritone!

What to bring: Blankets, lawn chairs, and picnic baskets are encouraged. Food trucks and beverage vendors will be available on-site.

Parking: Free parking is available at Pleasant Hill City Hall and surrounding areas.
