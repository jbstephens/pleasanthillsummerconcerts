About the Band

Van Goat is an Oakland five-piece that plays self-described "swing punk" — a joyful collision of big-band-era swing, ragtime, southern jazz, surf, and punk. Dual lead vocalists Aidan Ward (guitar/clarinet) and Lindsay Alexis (trombone) front a band whose sets leap from early-1900s classics to '50s and '60s hits to originals.

Veterans of Noise Pop and a 2019 European tour, they released the album Nobody Cares in 2021. They're also a returning favorite: their 2024 Sunset by the Lake set had the whole lawn singing along, and this is their third appearance in the series.

About This Concert

The 2026 Sunset by the Lake Summer Concert Series continues with Van Goat!

This concert features a Family Fun Zone with activities for kids including bounce houses and more—arrive early to enjoy the fun before the music starts!

What to bring: Blankets, lawn chairs, and picnic baskets are encouraged. Food trucks and beverage vendors will be available on-site.

Parking: Free parking is available at Pleasant Hill City Hall and surrounding areas.
